Build batch-resume fallback from batch_progress

When AI generation is re-enabled, some interrupted large batches only have
batch_progress stored on the schedule row rather than a resumable run_state.
Resume sweep now merges schedules with run_state and schedules with
batch_progress, and synthesises a resumable run_state from batch_progress when
appropriate so the existing resume logic can re-dispatch the remaining slices.
